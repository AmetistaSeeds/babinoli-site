About me

I’m a well-blended mix of many things: a creative soul, a conscious seeker, tuned in to technology and, at the same time, interested in environmental issues. That explains why I study Computer Science and Environmental Sciences — a long-term project.

Learning and teaching are my great passions: learning about myself and about everything around me, transforming the knowledge within and bringing it out.

Today I express myself through art, whether sung, written or painted. I have other professional projects, more technical ones; this one comes from the heart. It speaks of my essence and keeps my light shining.

I hope you enjoy what you find here. And of course, I’d love to hear a little about you too.
