Me and AI

Why I don’t hide that I use AI

Because you deserve to know where what reaches you comes from. Transparency is also a form of respect.

Throughout the site, you’ll see a little bar in which I’m represented by the heart and AI by the chip. It shows how much of the creation was decided by me. Whatever was generated by AI is always written next to it.

Key

  • Four red bars: all mine, from idea to execution.
  • One gray bar: all mine, with occasional help from AI, such as putting elements together or correcting grammar.
  • Two gray bars: AI generated parts of the result, but under my direction from start to finish. I provided the basis (lyrics, script, sketch), chose, corrected and redid each part until it was the way I wanted, and did the editing.
  • Three gray bars: I provided a sketch or a reference, and AI developed the result, with few adjustments from me.
  • Four gray bars: AI did the work; I gave the instructions and reviewed the result.

In a group of content, such as the legends, the bar refers to the content as a whole. If, however, there is a detail worth mentioning in specific parts, there will be a bar in that specific part.

Some examples

  • The Liebe Amazônia legends: four red bars. I illustrated each one and wrote the text of each legend. (The English translations were made with AI.)
  • “Meu Espaço Seguro”: two gray bars. The lyrics are entirely mine, and the vocals started from a recording of my voice; the melody, the arrangement and the final vocals were generated with AI.
  • The cover of “Meu Espaço Seguro”: two gray bars. I made the sketch of the pose and the prompt, chose the setting and sent photos of myself; the image went through several AIs and many rounds of adjustments until it was the way I wanted.
  • The “Meu Espaço Seguro” music video: two gray bars. The lyrics and the script are mine; I first made the entire video in 2D and then redid everything in 3D, directed every scene, redid images and videos until they were the way I wanted and edited the video. Images, videos and audio were made with AI; the Libras interpretation is by the interpreter Anaju.
  • The legends’ “Did you know?” sections: four gray bars. I asked for the text, AI wrote it, and I reviewed it.

I’m a Computer Engineering student. That’s why I use AI consciously. There is a symbiosis between us: we complete each other. It helps me with what I lack, and I help it with what it lacks. That’s how we seek the best result for you, who receive our content, giving credit, transparently, where it is due.
